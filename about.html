Our Journey of Healing

From a small clinic to a NABH-certified hospital — our story is rooted in compassion and excellence.

1979

Foundation

Dhaniram was established as a small Ayurvedic clinic in Hapur, with a vision to serve the community with traditional therapies.

1998

Expansion

Grew into a full-fledged hospital with 20 beds, introducing advanced Panchakarma and specialized departments.

2015

NABH Certification

Achieved NABH accreditation, setting new standards in patient safety, quality care, and clinical excellence.

2023

Digital Wellness

Launched tele-consultation and wellness programs, making Ayurveda accessible globally.

2026

Legacy Continues

Today, Dhaniram stands as a symbol of trust, with over 45 years of excellence and thousands of healed lives.

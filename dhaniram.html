The Gold Standard

Panchakarma — Complete Detoxification

Panchakarma is the cornerstone of Ayurvedic healing — a comprehensive detoxification and rejuvenation program that cleanses the body of accumulated toxins (Ama) and restores the natural balance of doshas.

  • Vamana — Therapeutic emesis to eliminate excess Kapha dosha, effective for respiratory and skin conditions.
  • Virechana — Purgation therapy to cleanse Pitta dosha, beneficial for liver, gallbladder, and digestive disorders.
  • Basti — Medicated enema therapy for Vata-related conditions like joint pain, arthritis, and neurological issues.
  • Nasya — Nasal administration of medicated oils to clear sinus, improve memory, and treat head & neck disorders.
  • Raktamokshana — Blood purification therapy for skin disorders, gout, and chronic inflammatory conditions.

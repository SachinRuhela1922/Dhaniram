Frequently Asked Questions

What is Panchakarma and how does it help? ▼
Panchakarma is a comprehensive detoxification and rejuvenation therapy in Ayurveda. It includes five procedures: Vamana, Virechana, Basti, Nasya, and Raktamokshana. These help eliminate deep-seated toxins, balance doshas, and restore optimal health.
Is Dhaniram a NABH accredited hospital? ▼
Yes, Dhaniram is a NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers) certified Ayurveda hospital. This accreditation ensures that we maintain the highest standards of patient safety, quality care, and clinical excellence.
Do you offer insurance cashless facilities? ▼
Yes, we have tie-ups with major insurance providers and offer both cashless and reimbursement options. Our team assists you with all insurance formalities to ensure a hassle-free experience.
How long does a typical treatment last? ▼
The duration varies depending on the condition and the treatment plan. Some therapies are short-term (7-14 days), while comprehensive Panchakarma programs can last 21-28 days. Our doctors will recommend the ideal duration based on your health needs.
